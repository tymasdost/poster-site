poster

poster is a private tool used by its owner to publish their own videos to their own YouTube channels and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy policy

The tool runs on the owner's computer. It accesses only the accounts its owner connects, and only to upload videos they choose. Access tokens are stored locally on that computer and are not sold or shared. Videos may be stored temporarily on a third-party file host while Instagram fetches them. Access can be revoked at any time in the Google account or Instagram settings.

Terms of service

The tool is provided as is, for its owner's own use, with no warranty. Use must comply with the terms of YouTube and Instagram.
